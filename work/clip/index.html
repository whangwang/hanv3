Color palette

Inspired by aurora, we choose this set of color to represent the young and progressive spirit of this product, and also the country where Snowbridge Inc. come from - Sweden.

Typography

In choosing typography, we worked toward the goal to emphasize the “Hi-Tech” impression.
Using Syncopate as a headline will surely be a strong hint to the user since it has wide body width and a unique appearance.
But when we talk about display information, a font like Syncopate won’t be a good idea, so that’s why we choose Red Hat Display and Red Hat Text for the title and paragraph, matching with our headline but also letting users feel comfortable reading the text in our app.
