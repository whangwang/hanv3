I participated in a design hackathon organized by CISSA, an organization at the University of Melbourne, and won first place with this project proposal.

The challenge was to design a service or product that would benefit a non-profit organisation. Over the course of just two days, I led a team of four designers through the entire process, including idea brainstorming, wireframing, interface design, and presentation preparation.

Our Mission

How can we utilise crowd-sourcing to support bird conservation efforts?

for Bird Lovers

In bird conservation, geolocation data plays a critical role. It’s essential for bird conservation organisations to collect and record the distribution of different bird species, enabling them to track, observe, and even organise bird-watching events to raise awareness about these creatures. However, identifying these small beings in the wild is no easy task.

At the same time, bird lovers face challenges in identifying birds, accessing timely information, and recording their sightings.

To tackle these challenges, we've proposed an app as the solution. It enables users to identify bird species by taking photos and incorporates gamification features to motivate them to log their sightings. This data can then be shared with non-profit organisations for future analysis and conservation initiatives.

Visual Design

Presenting nature’s elegance
with a modern touch

Colour Palette

Inspired by the colours found in nature, we chose a palette of green, blue, and a striking yellow-green gradient—sampled from seasonal leaves and the blue sky, the birds' natural stage.

To ensure the birds remain the focal point, we used a dark background throughout the app, allowing the photos to truly stand out. This colour palette creates a visual identity that is meaningful, vibrant, modern, and user-friendly.

Typography

To create a modern vibe while preserving a dictionary-like experience, we chose Yeseva One as our headline font—a serif typeface that blends elegance with energy.

While the distinctive headline font adds character and visual appeal, we paired it with a clean sans-serif font, Rubik, for paragraph text. Rubik ensures content remains accessible and easy to read. This thoughtful font pairing strikes a balance between aesthetics and usability, reinforcing both our brand identity and the overall user experience.

Illustration

Illustrations also play a key role in shaping the product’s tone. In our icon set, we adopted a line art style to maintain a clean and modern aesthetic. Gradient colors are applied consistently across all illustrations—from icons to badges.

Badges, in particular, are a core element in our app, designed to enhance the gamified experience. Through carefully crafted color gradients, subtle lighting, and defined borders, the badges emulate a tangible, collectible feel, making users' achievements feel more rewarding and real.
